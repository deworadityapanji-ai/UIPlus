# UI Plus (Beta)

UI Plus is a HUD overhaul for Nuclear Option. It comes with four preset HUD styles and an in-game editor that makes almost everything on screen customisable.

**Four presets**
- **Arcade:** a clean Ace Combat-inspired layout.
- **Wingman:** a slanted, Project Wingman-inspired layout, with the weapon star.
- **Dashboard:** every instrument in one strip along the bottom.
- **Thunder:** a War Thunder-inspired, text-first layout.

**Customise (almost) everything**
- Move, resize, rotate, show or hide each HUD element.
- Pick a skin per element: the weapon list, speed and altitude blocks, map, damage silhouette, targeting screen, RWR alerts, kill feed and more.
- Mix and match: add elements from another style as extras, such as Arcade's fuel tapes in Wingman, or Thunder's data block in Arcade.
- Every style keeps its own saved layout.
- Settings live in their own **UI PLUS** tab in the game's settings menu, with the editor one click away.
- A **Mod compatibility** box in that tab lists every supported mod: greyed out if it isn't installed, otherwise a switch plus whether the integration is working.

**Also included**
- The HUD in chase, cockpit and orbit views.
- Targeting camera feed on the HUD.
- Kill feed, mission banners and subtitles.
- Minimap zoom.
- **Hide HUD** hotkey: one press hides the whole HUD while flying, the next brings it back (bind "UI Plus Hide HUD" in Flight Controls).
- **Missile arcs** (Arcade, or any style as an extra): Ace Combat-style red arcs on a ring showing where incoming missiles come from, growing as they close in. Hidden in player-versus-player games.
- **High contrast:** a soft dark halo behind HUD text keeps it readable over bright sky (Off / Soft / Strong).
- **Steady HUD** (from SteadyHudMarkers by musket golfer): target markers no longer sway in third person, the HUD centre stays upright, and the compass can follow the camera.
- **Fix for a game bug:** the target arrow no longer points the wrong way when your target is behind you.
- With ArcadeCam installed: HIT / MISS / DESTROYED callouts.

**Out-of-the-box compatibility**
- **ArcadeCam** (optional camera companion).
- **KaceyTronic RWR:** the scope and alerts follow the HUD, and the alerts are movable on their own.
- **Ace Combat Style Reloads:** reserves and reload progress.
- **NeutralQoL** and **NOAutopilot:** their fuel overlays step aside when a style shows its own.
- **NO Missile Indicators:** its style follows the HUD style (Project Wingman's 3D indicator in Wingman, arrows elsewhere), and its arrows step aside where UI Plus's missile arcs show.
- **NuclearMinimap:** it runs the minimap and UI Plus leaves it to it; its cockpit map stays readable.
- **WSO Yappinator:** voice lines as subtitles. This needs a little work: each voice pack needs a dialogue text file for its lines to show.

**Beta**

UI Plus is in beta and will probably reach NOMM in December. Until then I'm gathering feedback. There's a good chance I've missed something or that a style doesn't behave somewhere, so bug reports, screenshots and layout ideas are very welcome.
